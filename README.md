# Healthcare-operation-analytics
# 🏥 Hospital Operations & Patient Care Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Hospital Operations & Patient Care Analytics Dashboard** designed to analyze patient volume, treatment costs, satisfaction, waiting time, insurance revenue, and doctor/department performance.

This dashboard provides a centralized view of key hospital KPIs and helps identify operational trends and patient-care insights.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform hospital operational data into meaningful and easy-to-understand visual insights.

The dashboard helps healthcare administrators and decision-makers monitor:

- Patient volume and trends
- Treatment and medicine costs
- Patient satisfaction
- Average length of stay
- Department performance
- Doctor-wise patient volume
- Insurance revenue
- Visit type distribution
- Average waiting time

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Patients by Month
A monthly trend analysis showing changes in patient volume throughout the year.

This helps identify:
- High and low patient-volume periods
- Monthly demand patterns
- Seasonal variations

### 2. Patients by Department
Shows the number of patients handled by each hospital department, including:

- Cardiology
- Emergency
- ICU
- Pediatrics
- General Medicine
- Dermatology
- Neurology
- Orthopedics
- ENT

### 3. Average Treatment Cost by Department
Compares the average treatment cost across departments and helps identify departments with higher treatment expenses.

### 4. Average Length of Stay by Diagnosis
Analyzes the average duration of patient stays for different diagnoses such as:

- Asthma
- Hypertension
- Infection
- Gastritis
- Pneumonia
- Diabetes
- Fracture
- Migraine
- Heart Disease

### 5. Total Bill by Insurance Type
Shows the total billing amount generated from different insurance categories:

- Corporate
- Government
- Private

### 6. Average Patient Satisfaction by Department
Compares patient satisfaction scores across hospital departments to identify areas with higher or lower patient experience.

### 7. Patients by Doctor
Provides doctor-wise patient volume to understand workload distribution among doctors.

### 8. Visit Type Analysis
Shows the distribution of patients based on visit type:

- Emergency
- IP (Inpatient)
- OP (Outpatient)

### 9. Average Waiting Time by Department
Compares the average waiting time across departments, helping identify departments where operational efficiency can be improved.
